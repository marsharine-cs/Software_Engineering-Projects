TEACHING APPROACH

Individualized Coding Instruction

For beginning learners, I use visual programming, playful challenges, and short demonstrations to build sequencing, loops, and problem-solving confidence. For students with prior experience, I use Python projects, debugging challenges, automation, and progressively independent builds.

Every learner starts with an interest and skills assessment; instruction includes guided practice, student explanations, and observable milestones.

TRANSFERABLE TRAINING SKILLS

Technical Learning & Software Enablement

I apply instructional design principles to technical learning: defined objectives, clear explanations, guided practice, useful documentation, feedback, and checks for understanding. My SaaS support and software-development experience helps me explain tools and workflows to learners with different levels of technical familiarity.

My public portfolio demonstrates transferable training capabilities; it does not represent a claim of having held a formal corporate trainer position.

RESPONSIBLE TECHNOLOGY

AI as a Learning Support

Students plan, write, test, and explain code themselves. AI may provide prompts, hints, or code-review questions, but is not used to replace the learner's reasoning. Students learn to verify suggestions and explain what their programs do.

Computer Science teaching credentials: New Jersey Certificate of Eligibility in Computer Science Technology and Florida computer science eligibility.
